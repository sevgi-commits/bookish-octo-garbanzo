Privacy policy

Raava personal is a private, personal-use application operated by a single individual (the owner) for her own Google account.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app accesses only the owner's own Google data: Google Calendar, Gmail (read-only) and Google Drive. It uses this data to produce the owner's reminders, summaries and planning notes.

What it does not do

The app does not collect, sell, share or publish data about any other person. It has no other users. It does not use Google data for advertising.

Where data is kept

Data is processed on a private server controlled by the owner and is not made available to third parties.

Contact

This is a personal project. Questions can be sent to the owner through the Google account that owns this project.
